Contoura is a non-invasive aesthetic treatment platform designed to address certain skin and body appearance concerns using radiofrequency (RF) technology. Its BodyFX and MiniFX applicators combine radiofrequency energy with vacuum massage to target selected treatment areas and temporarily reduce the appearance of cellulite. These technologies are designed to help improve the appearance of treated areas without surgical incisions.

Contoura may be considered by individuals who want to address visible cellulite or improve the appearance of specific body areas without undergoing an invasive procedure. Understanding how the technology works, what its applicators do, and what results are supported by clinical evidence can help patients make informed decisions before considering treatment.

## What Is Contoura?

Contoura is a non-invasive aesthetic workstation that uses radiofrequency technology and specialized applicators to address selected body appearance concerns, particularly the temporary reduction of visible cellulite.

The system includes different handpieces designed for specific treatment areas. BodyFX and MiniFX combine radiofrequency energy with mechanical vacuum massage to deliver controlled treatment to the skin and underlying tissues.

**Contoura may be considered for areas such as:**

- Abdomen
- Thighs
- Hips
- Buttocks
- Flanks or love handles
- Other selected areas suitable for the applicator

The treatment is designed for localized aesthetic concerns rather than significant overall weight loss.

Unlike surgical body contouring, Contoura treatments do not involve surgical fat removal. If you want to understand how different non-surgical technologies approach localized body concerns, explore our guide on [how non-invasive body sculpting works](/how-non-invasive-body-sculpting-works/).

## What Are the Benefits of Contoura?

Contoura offers a non-surgical approach to temporarily improving the appearance of cellulite, with treatment options designed for different body areas and minimal interruption to normal activities for many patients.

**Its potential advantages include:**

- Cellulite appearance: BodyFX and MiniFX are intended to temporarily reduce the appearance of cellulite in treated areas.
- Non-invasive treatment: The procedure does not require surgical incisions.
- Targeted application: Different applicators allow providers to treat larger and smaller areas.
- No general anesthesia: Treatment does not ordinarily require general anesthesia.
- Convenience: Sessions can generally be scheduled as outpatient aesthetic treatments.
- Limited recovery requirements: Many patients can resume regular activities shortly after treatment.

It is important to distinguish these potential advantages from guaranteed outcomes. Individual improvements vary, and Contoura should not be described as a replacement for surgical fat removal or a guaranteed method of eliminating wrinkles and loose skin.

## What Skin and Body Concerns Can Contoura Address?

Contoura's BodyFX and MiniFX handpieces are intended to provide temporary improvement in the appearance of cellulite. Patients may consider treatment when they want to improve the appearance of uneven skin texture in selected areas.

However, cellulite reduction, fat reduction, and skin tightening are different aesthetic goals. A treatment intended to improve the appearance of cellulite does not automatically establish that it can achieve significant fat loss or correct substantial skin laxity.

For individuals primarily concerned about loose skin, other treatments may be more appropriate. Our guide to [skin tightening treatments](/skin-tightening/) explains different approaches to improving skin texture and firmness.

## How Does Contoura Work?

Contoura works by combining radiofrequency energy with vacuum-assisted mechanical massage to deliver controlled treatment to selected skin and underlying tissue areas.

The radiofrequency technology generates energy within the treatment area, while the vacuum component creates suction that helps the applicator maintain contact with the skin.

The system also monitors treatment parameters to support controlled energy delivery.

## How Does Radiofrequency Energy Work?

Radiofrequency energy uses electromagnetic energy to produce controlled effects within tissue.

In aesthetic applications, radiofrequency technology can be used for different purposes depending on the device design, energy settings, and treatment method.

With Contoura's BodyFX and MiniFX handpieces, radiofrequency is combined with vacuum massage. This approach is designed for selected treatment applications, including temporary improvement in the appearance of cellulite.

It is important not to confuse this mechanism with surgical fat removal. The treatment does not physically suction fat out of the body like liposuction.

### How Does Vacuum Massage Contribute to Treatment?

The vacuum component creates controlled suction against the skin during treatment.

This mechanical action works alongside the radiofrequency delivery to provide the combined treatment approach used by BodyFX and MiniFX.

The system also incorporates monitoring features that help the provider observe treatment parameters during the procedure.

According to [InMode's Contoura device information](https://www.inmodemd.com/edgar_pdf/zk2023956.pdf) in its regulatory filing, the BodyFX and MiniFX applicators are indicated for temporary reduction in the appearance of cellulite, along with certain other specified applications.

## What Is BodyFX?

BodyFX is the larger Contoura applicator designed for treating broader body areas using a combination of radiofrequency energy and vacuum massage.

Its design makes it suitable for selected areas where a larger treatment surface is useful.

Potential treatment areas include:

- Abdomen
- Thighs
- Buttocks
- Flanks
- Other appropriate body areas

During treatment, the applicator is placed against the skin to deliver the combined RF and vacuum technology.

The provider moves the handpiece across the designated treatment zones according to the device's treatment protocol and the patient's tolerance.

The purpose is to provide controlled treatment across the selected area rather than targeting a single tiny spot.

## What Is MiniFX?

MiniFX is a smaller Contoura applicator designed for more localized treatment areas where a compact handpiece provides greater targeting flexibility.

It uses the same general combination of radiofrequency energy and vacuum massage as BodyFX, but its smaller design makes it suitable for selected smaller treatment zones.

Depending on the treatment plan and device protocol, areas may include:

- Smaller areas of the abdomen
- Knees
- Selected areas of the thighs
- Other localized areas suitable for the applicator

The provider determines which applicator is appropriate based on the treatment area, anatomy, and individual goals.

BodyFX and MiniFX are not interchangeable in every situation. The size of the treatment area and the intended application help determine which handpiece should be used.

## Is Contoura Safe, and Who Is a Suitable Candidate?

Contoura may be appropriate for selected patients seeking non-invasive cellulite improvement, but suitability depends on medical history, the treatment area, and the device's safety requirements.

Although the treatment does not involve surgical incisions, radiofrequency and vacuum-based procedures still require appropriate patient screening and trained operation.

The Contoura operator manual identifies medical conditions and circumstances that may make treatment unsuitable or require additional precautions.

These include:

- Pregnancy or nursing.
- Pacemakers, internal defibrillators, or other active electrical implants.
- Certain permanent implants in the treatment area.
- Active skin conditions such as sores, eczema, or rashes in the treatment area.
- Certain serious medical conditions.
- Some immune system disorders or medications affecting immune function.
- The complete contraindications should be reviewed by the treating clinician before proceeding.

According to the [FDA's explanation of the 510(k) clearance process](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/premarket-submissions-selecting-and-preparing-correct-submission/premarket-notification-510k), a 510(k) clearance establishes substantial equivalence to a legally marketed device for specified intended uses. It should not be confused with a general guarantee of safety or effectiveness for every aesthetic claim.

Patients should discuss the device's intended use, potential adverse effects, and their personal medical history before starting treatment.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: 1. Is Contoura a Safe Treatment?

Contoura is a medical aesthetic system with monitoring features intended to support controlled treatment. However, no procedure is entirely risk-free. Proper patient screening, trained operation, and adherence to the manufacturer's instructions are important.

### Q: 2. Does Contoura Treatment Hurt?

Many patients describe the treatment sensation as manageable. During a session, you may experience warmth and a pulling or suction sensation as the radiofrequency and vacuum components operate.

Individual comfort levels vary depending on the treatment area and settings.

### Q: 3. How Many Contoura Sessions Are Required?

The number of sessions depends on the treatment area, individual goals, and the provider's assessment. The original treatment information describes a weekly treatment schedule over approximately eight weeks, but this should not be treated as a universal requirement for every patient.

Your provider should recommend a schedule based on the applicable device protocol and your response to treatment.

### Q: 4. What Is the Post-Procedure Care for Contoura?

Contoura is a non-invasive treatment and generally does not require the type of recovery associated with surgery.

However, patients may experience temporary redness or other skin reactions after treatment. The operator manual recommends avoiding very hot water for two days and avoiding scrubbing or pinching the treated area during that period.

Follow the specific aftercare instructions provided by your clinician.

### Q: 5. Can Contoura Remove Stubborn Fat?

Contoura's BodyFX and MiniFX handpieces are indicated for temporary improvement in the appearance of cellulite. They should not be presented as equivalent to surgical fat removal or as a guaranteed treatment for significant localized fat reduction.

Patients seeking substantial fat removal should discuss other options with a qualified provider.

### Q: 6. Is Contoura a Weight-Loss Treatment?

No. Contoura is an aesthetic treatment platform, not a substitute for a medically supervised weight-loss program. Its intended cellulite-related application focuses on improving the appearance of selected treated areas rather than producing significant overall weight loss.

## Conclusion

Contoura is a non-invasive aesthetic platform that uses radiofrequency energy and vacuum massage through its BodyFX and MiniFX applicators. These handpieces provide treatment options for different body areas and are intended, among other specified applications, to temporarily reduce the appearance of cellulite.

Although the technology offers an alternative to invasive procedures for selected aesthetic concerns, it is important to maintain realistic expectations. Contoura should not be described as a guaranteed fat-removal treatment, a substitute for liposuction, or a proven solution for every form of loose skin and wrinkles.

Understanding the device's intended applications, potential limitations, and safety requirements can help patients make more informed decisions. A consultation with a qualified provider is the best way to determine whether Contoura is appropriate for your individual goals.
